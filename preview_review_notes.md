# Runway Fuel preview review notes

The rendered homepage successfully expresses the intended Operational Ledger direction. The hero reads as high-trust and editorial, with strong type hierarchy, an effective split layout, and brand-consistent use of navy and amber. The operating-layer section and public-functions cards render clearly and maintain a disciplined, non-generic presentation.

The diagrams display correctly, the sticky navigation is visible, and the lower sections retain coherent spacing and visual continuity. The public-functions section in particular reads cleanly and benefits from the restrained card treatment. The site is suitable for packaging in its current state.

A future enhancement could replace the frontend-only contact preparation behavior with a live routing endpoint, but that is not required for the current GitHub and Vercel-ready release.
